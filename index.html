01 // Test Strategy & Resource Planning

STRATEGIZE

Quality starts long before test execution. I work with Business Analysts, Product Owners, and Solution Architects in process walkthroughs to clarify complex payment rules, Provider/Proxy/CRT workflows, GST, invoicing, and migration scope—challenging unclear requirements upfront rather than embedding assumptions into the test suite.

02 // Traceability & Scenario Architecture

ARCHITECT

Rather than testing isolated acceptance criteria in silos, I design scenario-based functional, integration, negative, boundary, and recovery suites in Azure DevOps—linking every Build, User Story, Acceptance Criterion, Test Case, Execution result, and Defect into an auditable Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM).

03 // Deep Technical Validation & Automation

VALIDATE

Combining team leadership with hands-on execution: driving SQL source-to-target data reconciliation, validating multi-hop payment journeys (Batch Portal → Batch API → QuickPay → Windcave & Datacom 3DS 2.0), auditing WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility with NVDA, and building repeatable E2E automation in Cypress, Mabl, and Selenium.

04 // Multi-Vendor & 5-Person Team Governance

GOVERN

Leading day-to-day delivery across a five-person QA team and international vendors (COEXYA in France, US development partners). I build interactive QA operating dashboards that separate build-level readiness from future scope, giving executives clear visibility of planned vs. executed cases, P1/P2 defects, and residual risk.

05 // Release Readiness & Cutover Assurance

RELEASE
